chisel

Privacy

Your journal stays yours.

What Chisel stores, and where

Everything you write or record is saved to your own Google Drive, in a folder called Chisel: a Google Doc per month, audio files for voice notes, and a small index file (chisel-index.json) that powers the list view. Chisel has no server and no database of its own.

What Chisel can access

Chisel asks for the drive.file permission, which only lets it see and change files it created. It cannot read anything else in your Drive. It also reads your name, email address and profile picture to show who is signed in.

Voice transcription

When “Transcribe” is on, your browser’s built-in speech recognition turns speech into text. In some browsers (such as Chrome) that audio is processed by the browser vendor’s speech service. Turn it off to keep audio entirely between your device and your Drive.

Signing out

Signing out revokes Chisel’s access token. You can remove Chisel’s access at any time from your Google Account. Deleting the Chisel folder removes all of your entries.
